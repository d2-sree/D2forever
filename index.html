Dearest Dinesh,

If someone had told me during our first year of college — as we stood awkwardly in that noisy, crowded Xerox shop queue — that the boy standing near me would become my whole world, I would have smiled in disbelief.

From sharing notes and becoming friends, to the comfort of becoming each other’s best friend, you slowly slipped into every fiber of my routine. And then came October 23, 2023 — the day you took my hands and asked me to walk beside you. Saying yes to you was the easiest, truest decision my heart has ever made.

Do you remember October 31, 2023? Our very first date at Thiruthani temple, climbing those stone steps together with prayer in our hearts? That sacred hill witnessed our promises, and every train ride from Nemilichery to Avadi since then has felt like home because you were sitting beside me.

I love the evenings we waited on Avadi station benches, refusing to let the hours end. I love holding onto you tightly on your Splendor bike, feeling safe against the rush of Chennai traffic. I love our silly walks at Avadi lake park, laughing like children on park swings, and how a simple plate of fried rice with you always felt like the grandest feast on earth.

We’ve had our share of misunderstandings, little arguments, and countless fights. But in every storm, we held tighter. You are my safe harbor, my favorite laughter, my greatest supporter, and my deepest love.

Thank you for three magical, unforgettable years. Through every tomorrow, every twist of fate, and every chapter yet unwritten... I would still choose you. Every single time.

Forever & always yours, With all my love ❤️
